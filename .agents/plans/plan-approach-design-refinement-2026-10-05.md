# Approach design refinement

Objective: Make checkpoint selection feel like expanding the selected step within the existing journey.
Scope: Approach component, section styles and animation only.
Tasks: Keep the journey navigation visible, replace overview summaries with one detailed view, simplify typography and controls, animate only changing content.
Acceptance: A single detail view replaces the overview; the path retains context; selection is clear; keyboard focus and reduced motion work; layout adapts to mobile.
Risks: Excessive empty space and losing the visual connection to the selected step. Keep the path persistent and use content-driven sizing.
Success: A quieter, cohesive design with a direct relationship between checkpoint and explanation.
